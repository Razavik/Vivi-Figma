# Darkroom
A print room with the lights off: warm black ground a step lighter than a print's own black, so a photograph sits on it as an object; fibre-white ink; hairline extra-condensed capitals; one amber safelight. Photographs carry the weight, type the rest.

## Palette
- paper #090604 (ground) | paper-2 #100c0a (well) | paper-3 #1b1613 (surface)
- ink #e9e4dc | ink-2 #c2bdb5 | muted #918b85 | rule #2f2a27
- accent amber #ec8d38 (the one filled control, selected plate, a number; never a wash, band, glow or head) | accent-deep #934e1e (quieter fill) | accent-ink #090604
Balance: dark ground in lightness steps; amber scarce; max two bands (paper-2 well with grain), never an amber field. Alt colourways: red signal, or fibre-paper light ground (#f2efe9-like), only on request.

## Type
- Display: extra-condensed capitals; weight 200 for hero, section heads, figures (hero up to poster size, 120-200, lh 1.0, ls -0.5%); weight 400 for item titles and controls. Never bolder for heads.
- Body: Barlow regular 17, lh 1.6; light 300 only at lead size or above.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 0 everywhere; no shadows, vignette or bloom. Depth = lightness steps only.
- Rules 1px #2f2a27 full width between rows; vertical hairlines between figures.

## Controls
- Primary: fill #ec8d38, text #090604, extra-condensed regular caps 20, radius 0, h48. Secondary: 1px #e9e4dc outline.

## Range
- Figures as an edition: real numbers in hairline 200 at 190px+ on a grained well, vertical hairlines between, muted Barlow line beneath each; colour stays ink.
- Collector's sheet: full-width hairline rows, muted label left, value in display caps right (series, paper, edition, sizes).

## Voice
- Lab notes and a collector's sheet: series, plate, place, month, edition, paper; exact numbers, short lines.
- Never: amber wash, band or glow; sprocket holes, film strips or grease-pencil rings; bold heads; sepia or tinted pictures; flat screen black under prints.
