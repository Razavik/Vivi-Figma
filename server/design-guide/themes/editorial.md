# Editorial
A contemporary magazine voice: warm white uncoated stock, a printed black with a cool tilt, squeezed heavy sans heads, serif for reading and the standfirst, square corners, a heavy ink rule over hairlines, one printer's green.

## Palette
- paper #f7f5f0 | paper-2 #eeebe4 (well) | paper-3 #e5e1d9
- ink #0c0d12 | ink-2 #2a2b31 (body) | muted #615d57 | rule #d0cdc7 | rule-2 #8f8c85
- accent printer's green #007439 (one tag, hover, focus; under 5%) | accent-ink #faf8f5
Balance: paper and ink; max two bands (paper-2 well, one ink band with paper type); never a green band or a green rule.

## Type
- Display: condensed extrabold, sentence case; hero 96-160, lh 0.92; section heads 56-72, lh 1.0; ls -1.2%. Never a serif head.
- Body: serif regular 18, lh 1.55, ink-2; italic only for quotations. Standfirst: serif 24-44, lh 1.2 at the large end, ink.
- Labels, captions, controls, table heads, bylines: sans medium 13-14, sentence case, captions muted. Figures in the display face, lining tabular. No mono.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 0 everywhere; no shadows, no texture.
- Rules: 4px ink opens a section or table with the head flush under it; 1px #d0cdc7 hairlines between rows/columns; #8f8c85 under a table head.

## Controls
- Primary: fill #0c0d12, text #f7f5f0, sans medium 15, radius 0, h48. Secondary: 1px ink outline. One tag may be a filled green rectangle.

## Range
- Contents as rows: under the 4px rule, 3-6 hairline rows, title in the condensed head ~40 left, serif standfirst right, page/date at the far edge.
- Standfirst as statement: one or two serif sentences at 36-44 across ten columns under the heavy rule.

## Voice
- Contemporary, lightly literary, exact: issue, page count, date; heads read like cover lines, standfirsts give the argument in one breath.
- Never: serif heads; the default-width sans head; rounded corners, pills or shadows; two picture sections in a row; all-caps shouting.
