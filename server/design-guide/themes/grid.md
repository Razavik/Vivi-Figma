# Grid
Swiss international style: a white sheet, blue-black ink, one neo-grotesque, and two flat colours with separate jobs. Structure comes from column lines; no strokes part sections and nothing is boxed.

## Palette
- paper #F8F7F3 (page) | paper-2 #EDECE7 (wells) | paper-3 #E2DFDA
- ink #0F141E (type, primary control) | ink-2 #2C303B | muted #575B64 | rule #D8D6D0
- field/accent #1F40BE (ultramarine: the flood a sheet is printed on) | field-ink #F8F7F3
- mark #D9241B (Swiss red: the one chosen thing; at most one red field per page) | mark-ink #F8F7F3 | focus #2347C5
Balance: white paper plus two or three full-edge fields. Red never on ultramarine; two fields never touch.

## Type
- Display: Radio Canada Big bold, sentence case, flush left, ragged right, never centred. Poster 120-160 at lineHeight 0.9, letterSpacing -4.5%; section 48-72 at 1.0, -3.5%. One weight and colour per head; a head crossing onto a field switches to field-ink at the field's edge.
- Figures and index letters in the display cut, may exceed any head; a statement sets in display regular.
- Body: Radio Canada regular 17, lineHeight 1.5.
- Labels/captions/nav: Radio Canada medium 13-14, sentence case, untracked; muted on paper, field-ink on a field. No mono, no capitals.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- radius 0 everywhere; shadow none; no strokes, except 1px #D8D6D0 hairlines between table rows and under inputs.
- depth is scale and colour; a well is paper-2 without border.
- photos: square, edges on column lines, never under type. No texture.

## Controls
- primary: square fill #0F141E, text #F8F7F3, h 52, padding [0,24], medium; same on paper or any field.
- secondary: text with 1px underline (mark red when active).

## Range
- Poster head hung from column 1 running across a field that starts at a later column; letters turn field-ink where they cross; a large figure at the field's foot.
- An index as a specimen: letters or years at display size in a row, the chosen one in mark red repeated at full height beside it.

## Voice
- Exact and plain, sentence case; name record, year and press as a catalogue does; no adjectives.
- Never: a rule above a section, stroke around a control, boxed card; red on ultramarine, a second blue; lowercase or all-caps heads; colour or framed photos, or type over a photo; centred heads.
