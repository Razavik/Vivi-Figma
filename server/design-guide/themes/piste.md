# Piste
A mountain morning in print: snow-white paper, cold near-black ink, loud condensed capitals for every head and figure, square edges on 2px ink rules, and one cobalt doing all the colour work, from the primary control to a full field. Crisp full-colour photographs cut square.

## Palette
- paper #F8FAFE (page, the snow) | paper-2 #EDF0F6 | paper-3 #DDE1E8
- ink #0A0D12 | ink-2 #2A2E35 | muted #585E67 | rule #D4D8DE
- accent/field #1F50F5 (cobalt: primary, link hover, figures on paper, full bands) | accent-deep #1633B1 (hover/pressed) | accent-ink #F8FAFD
- focus #DF2321 (red, the focus ring and nothing else)
Balance: snow paper with ink and one cobalt; max two bands: a cobalt field with paper type, or paper-2 under a form/dense table.

## Type
- Display: Barlow Condensed extra-bold, UPPERCASE, roman, letterSpacing -0.5%; section heads 64-96, hero up to 128; lineHeight 1.0 for heads that wrap, 0.9 only for single unbreakable lines.
- Figures: same face, tabular, 96-200; cobalt on paper, paper on the field.
- Body: Barlow regular/medium 17-19, lineHeight 1.5; never lighter (read outdoors).

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- radius 0 on everything; shadow none; no gradient except a scrim; no blur or texture.
- rules: 2px ink above a list, figure row or table head; 1px #D4D8DE between rows. Ruled, never boxed.
- raised surface = paper step or the cobalt field.
- photos: square with 1px inside stroke ink at 8%.

## Controls
- primary: square fill #1F50F5, text #F8FAFD, condensed caps 18, h 52, padding [0,28]; hover #1633B1; active ink.
- secondary: 2px inset ink stroke (paper on a scrim or field).

## Range
- A figure row as a lift board: numbers up to 200 in cobalt on snow, under a 2px ink rule and split by hairlines, each with one Barlow line.
- A statement on the field: one short line of condensed caps at display size, paper on cobalt edge to edge.

## Voice
- The morning report: the hour, the group, the week booked. Never a slogan about freedom or powder.
- Never: lowercase or light condensed display; rounded corners, pills or soft cards; a second accent hue, cobalt on running text, or cobalt card fills on snow; sky gradients, sunset grades, or a drawn mountain, snowflake or piste map.
