# Atelier
A maker's catalogue in still air: limewash paper with a soft luminous haze, deep umber ink, light serif heads, hairlines, one slate signal nearly as dark as the ink. Things the house hands you (card, print, sample strip) are cotton stock lit from the top left.

## Palette
- paper #f1ece6 (page) | paper-2 #e9e3db | paper-3 #ded6cd
- ink #1c110a | ink-2 #382e28 (body) | muted #615953 | rule #c6c0b9 | pencil #6d6863
- accent slate #264d71 (underlines, drop cap; under 4% of the frame) | accent-ink #f5f1ec
- card #f9f7f2 (cotton) | card-edge #d6d0c9 | lit #fcf9f5 | stains (strip tips): #c98b90 #869a77 #a4744f #78a1a6
Balance: paper everywhere, umber ink, slate as a whisper; haze = a soft pale-amber/cream radial gradient (blur) behind the hero only.

## Type
- Display: serif light, hero 72-104 / lh 1.05 / ls -1.8%; statements 40-56 / ls -2.2%; sentence case, one weight, one colour.
- Small serif: regular 18-24 for h3 and card names; never a paragraph.
- Body: Hanken Grotesk regular 17, lh 1.6, ink-2. Labels: Hanken regular 14, ink object + muted material.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 0; cards 2. Hairline 1px #c6c0b9 on rows; tables open on a 1px ink rule.
- Card stock: fill #f9f7f2, 1px #d6d0c9 stroke, inner hairline frame inset 14px, shadow {0,8,14,#4d3a2e24} plus {14,36,64,#4d3a2e4d}; lit edge = 1px #fffefb top/left.

## Controls
- Primary: transparent, 1px ink stroke, ink text Hanken 15, radius 3, padding [14,28]; no filled control.

## Range
- Statement: one prompt sentence in light serif across 9-10 columns, a pencilled edition line, a short paragraph with a slate drop cap.
- Collection as blotter strips: narrow cotton tabs on a rail with a stain-coloured tip; chosen one dropped.
- Timetable: ruled table under an ink rule, names in serif, closed row muted.
- Booking as letterpress card on cotton: ringed day picker, chips, ruled lines.

## Voice
- Hand-set, object-precise: the object, the material, the number made, open days; no "shop now"/"discover".
- Never: a warm accent; bold or high-contrast serif heads; serif running text; two still lifes side by side at one size.
