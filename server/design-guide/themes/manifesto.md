# Manifesto
A declaration on a dark sheet: warm near-black ground, warm off-white ink, condensed capitals as big as the width allows and leaded tight, a small plain body, hairlines, one heavy ink bar, square edges and one fighting red. The force is poster-size caps against quiet text, with red spent sparingly.

## Palette
- paper #0A0704 (ground) | paper-2 #130E0B | paper-3 #1F1915
- ink #F3EEE4 | ink-2 #CFCAC2 | muted #938A82 | rule #362F29
- accent #DB0310 (red: primary block, one rule, the one red field) | accent-deep #B7010B (hover) | accent-text #F53E36 (the red word, link hover, focus) | accent-ink #FBFAF7
Balance: dark ground, off-white type; red under 5% of a screen outside the one inverted red field. Max two bands: paper-2 or the red field.

## Type
- Display: Anton-style condensed, regular, UPPERCASE only, letterSpacing 0.4%. Hero/statement 160-208 filling the width, lineHeight 0.9 when each line is its own text node; any wrapping head lineHeight 1.02. Never under 64px at 1440.
- Body: Public Sans regular/semibold, ink-2.
- Labels/captions/table heads/dates: Public Sans medium one step smaller, sentence case, untracked, muted. No mono.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- radius 0; shadow none; no cards, no lift.
- rules: 1px #362F29 between rows and under heads; one 4px ink bar under the nav or over a record.
- well: paper-2 field under a hairline. No texture, no ornament.
- photos: square, 1px #362F29 stroke, full-bleed or not at all; a head crosses one only over its darkest third on a #0A0704 plate; red never touches a picture.

## Controls
- primary: square red block #DB0310, text #FBFAF7, h 52, padding [0,28]; hover #B7010B. On the red field it turns ground colour with ink text.
- secondary: square, 1px ink-2 stroke, inverts to red on hover.

## Range
- A record: full-width rows under hairlines, numeral in the condensed face at ~48 in muted, the clause at ~32, one plain line beside.
- A poster line: one date, figure or slogan in the condensed face across all twelve columns.

## Voice
- Imperative, short, declarative; verbs over adjectives; no hedging.
- Never: a second accent; red flood beyond the one inverted surface; a light ground; ornament (stickers, torn edges, offset shadows, colour or red-toned photos); a serif, lowercase or title-case display.
