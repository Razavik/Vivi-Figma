# Garden
A grower's voice: warm oat paper, near-black green ink, serif heads, hairlines, soft moss and clay tints, daylight photographs. Every neutral leans green or oat; nothing boxed, glossy or grey.

## Palette
- paper #F7F1DF (page) | paper-2 #EEE6D1 (wells) | paper-3 #E3DAC2
- ink #072311 | ink-2 #223C28 | muted #4D5D4C | rule #C7C9B8 | rule-2 #8C9F86
- accent #1F6A19 (leaf green: primary, one figure; under 5% of a screen) | accent-2 #C3DBA6 (pale leaf, replaces accent on ink bands) | accent-ink #F8F5EA
- tint-moss #1F6A19 at 11% alpha | tint-clay #AE4F17 at 10% alpha (clay only as a tint band, never on controls, nav or heads)
Balance: oat paper dominant; at most two bands (moss tint, paper-2, or ink #072311 with oat text); never a solid leaf-green field.

## Type
- Display: Vollkorn semibold only (never italic or bold), hero 56-96, lineHeight 1.04, letterSpacing -1.2%, sentence case. Big figures (acreage, counts) 160px+ in the display serif.
- Body: Hanken Grotesk regular/medium 16-18, lineHeight 1.55; table numbers lining.
- Photo captions: Newsreader regular 13-14, muted, one fact per line.
- Labels/meta/dates: Hanken Grotesk medium 12-14, sentence case, muted, untracked. No mono.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- radius: wells and photos 4, controls pill 999.
- rules: 1px #C7C9B8 between rows; #8C9F86 tops a table or outlines a control.
- shadow: none. A well is paper-2 or a tint, never a bordered card. No texture, no gradients.
- photos: 4px radius, 1px #C7C9B8 inside stroke.

## Controls
- primary: flat pill, fill #1F6A19, text #F8F5EA, padding [12,24], medium.
- secondary: pill, 1px #8C9F86 stroke, ink text.

## Range
- Figures at 160px+ in the serif, three across on hairlines, a Hanken line beneath each.
- A season strip: twelve months in one hairline row, open months on the moss tint.

## Voice
- Warm, honest, seasonal, unhurried; field, variety, month, counts only as given.
- Never: grey-sage or slate paper; cool teal or corporate emerald; italic or faux-bold serif; clay on controls or heads; staged picnic or produce on white; "artisanal"/"curated" as hollow badges.
