# Solstice
An almanac's warmth: cream paper, warm brown ink, fat serif heads, gold hairlines doing the ruling, square surfaces on hard paper shadows, one vermilion signal. Seasonal through ink, gold and light, not drawings of produce; not terracotta-on-cream.

## Palette
- paper #f6e9d5 (page) | paper-2 #fbf4e6 (wells, bands) | paper-3 #ecd8b6 (hard shadow)
- ink #3a1e0e | ink-2 #523525 | muted #78553c | rule #e0c69f
- accent #c9212e (vermilion, one signal, under 5% of a screen) | accent-ink #fbf4e6
- gold #de9c31 | gold-2 #f0c374 (hairlines only, never fills or text)
Balance: cream and brown; two bands at most, paper-2 or one brown #3a1e0e band with cream text; never a vermilion field.

## Type
- Display: Yeseva One regular, hero 72-104, lineHeight 1.0, letterSpacing -0.5%, one colour. Figures too.
- Body: Archivo 16-17px, lineHeight 1.55, ink-2. Small print: Archivo Narrow semibold 13px for dates, captions, axis labels.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Square everywhere except buttons and stepper (pill). Rules: 2px ink bar atop a section; 1px #e0c69f between rows; gold hairline under a head in a well.
- Raised well: 2px ink border, 1px gold rule inset 6px, hard shadow {10,10,0,#ecd8b6}. Never a soft shadow. No texture.

## Controls
- Primary: vermilion pill, text #fbf4e6, Narrow semibold, padding [14,28].
- Secondary: ink text over a 2px ink underline, no box. Input: square, fill #fbf4e6, 1px ink stroke.

## Range
- Product in the light: cut-out bottle with a live-text label on a cream-to-dusk gradient, low warm glow.
- Label as ruled list: paper-2 well, gold-inset hairline, small-print labels left, serif values right.
- Year on one line: stages as stops on a hairline across the brown band, months in gold small print.

## Voice
The almanac: the hour of light, the pressing day, the orchard by name.
- Never a second warm colour.
- Never a grey-black ink.
- Never a rounded card or soft shadow; never clay in place of vermilion.
