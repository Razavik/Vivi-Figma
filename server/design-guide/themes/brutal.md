# Brutal
A raw engineered sheet: near-white poster stock, near-black ink as 3px rules, boxed cells and solid fills, blackest capitals, zero radius, one acid yellow that only ever fills. The grid is drawn: cells share rules, and a big word or number in a box is the ornament.

## Palette
- paper #f8f7f2 | paper-2 #ecebe6 | paper-3 #dedcd7
- ink #040302 | ink-2 #0e0d0a | muted #494843 | rule #040302
- accent acid yellow #f2e309 (fills only: chosen option, highlight, current row; under 5%; never type, rule or band) | accent-ink #040302
Balance: light sheet with black structure; max two bands (ink with paper text, or paper-2 ruled 3px top and bottom). Never black paper.

## Type
- Display: Libre Franklin black 900, uppercase, hero 96-160, lh 1.0, ls -3%; fill the measure rather than sit politely inside it.
- Body: regular 17-18, lh 1.5, sentence case; item titles and controls bold 700. Weights: 400/700/900 only.
- Numbers: black, tabular, section size or larger. Labels: regular 14, muted.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 0 everywhere. Two rules only: 3px ink boxes wells/tables/cells/photos; 1px ink splits rows inside. Neighbouring cells share one 3px rule, never two.
- Shadows, gradients, texture: none. Depth = border and fill.

## Controls
- Primary: solid ink fill, paper text, bold 16, radius 0, h48-52. Secondary: paper with 3px ink border.
- Input: h52, 3px ink border, paper fill. Chosen option fills yellow under ink text.

## Range
- Index as boxed table: one 3px box, number in black at section size in its own cell, entry bold, one regular line; current row yellow.
- Statement: one blunt sentence in black caps across 12 columns on a 3px rule.
- Bento of cells: words/numbers in black caps in boxed cells sharing rules, one double-size lead, one ink cell, a photo flush in one cell.
- Form as grid: boxed field cells, two big word cells that fill yellow when chosen, an ink send cell.

## Voice
- Blunt, declarative; caps in heads, sentence case elsewhere; say it flat, no hype.
- Never: rounded corners or any shadow; ornaments or offset stacks; black paper; a second colour; doubled 6px seams.
