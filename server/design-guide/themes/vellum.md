# Vellum
An old printing house: parchment ground, umber ink, gothic heads, a roman sibling for one large sentence, a plain grotesk for everything read, hairlines and double rules, and ultramarine as the one pigment.

## Palette
- paper #f3eada (page) | paper-2 #e9dcc7 (band with fibre wash) | paper-3 #decfb7
- ink #261a12 | ink-2 #3a2d24 | muted #62554a | rule #bcab96
- accent #2942ab (ultramarine: primary, list numerals, figures, one band) | accent-deep #1b2e87 | accent-soft #d1dbf3 (row wash) | accent-ink #f5eee0
Balance: parchment and umber, one ultramarine; bands paper-2 and one ultramarine field, two at most, never touching.

## Type
- Display: Grenze Gotisch semibold, only >=30px, hero 80-128, lineHeight 0.92, letterSpacing -1%. Below 30px use Hanken semibold. Never open a head on capital I.
- Statement/figures: Grenze regular roman, one sentence at ~40; figures at display size.
- Body: Hanken Grotesk 16-17px, lineHeight 1.55; labels medium a step smaller; table figures tabular, right-aligned.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 2, no pills, no shadows. Rules: 1px #bcab96 between rows; 1px ink atop a section; double ink rule (two 1px lines, 3px gap) over table heads and band tops.
- Fibre wash: long tan fibre pattern or soft tan gradient at 15-25% on a band or well only.

## Controls
- Primary: fill #2942ab, text #f5eee0, radius 2, Hanken medium, padding [14,24].
- Secondary: 1px ink stroke. Input: radius 2, 1px ink stroke.

## Range
- Day book table: ruled table under a double rule, ultramarine numerals indexing rows, name semibold, tabular values.
- Statement with figures: one roman sentence across 9-10 columns on the fibre band, real numbers beneath in gothic at display size in ultramarine.
- Rows as an index: 3-5 ruled rows, title in gothic at section size, one Hanken line beside.

## Voice
Ledger-plain and dated: item, weight, year, town. Never "artisanal" or "curated".
- Never clay or oxblood as the pigment.
- Never the gothic below 30px or at paragraph length.
- Never deckled edge, vignette or coffee ring.
