# Riso
A small-press run from two drums on bright uncoated stock: a medium blue that sets every word and a fluorescent pink for fills, crossing in a purple nobody mixed. Grain where the ink skips, one word a hair out of register; never black, not clean vector flatness.

## Palette
- paper #f8f5ed (page) | paper-2 #fee1ea (light pink screen, under text) | paper-3 #ffaccd (half screen, display type only)
- ink #1a3c9f (blue drum: all words, rules, icons) | ink-2 #2b4ea7 | muted #4360a4 | rule #bfceec
- accent #fe419d (pink drum: fills only) | accent-ink #061b64 | focus #1a41be
- purple only where pink overlaps blue (pink layer on multiply)
Balance: no black or grey; blue writes, pink fills; two bands at most (solid blue with stock-colour type, or solid pink with #061b64 type).

## Type
- Display: Cabinet Grotesk extra-bold (Inter bold), hero 72-112, lineHeight 0.92, letterSpacing -2.5%, sentence case, blue. Offer figures in it.
- Misregistered word: one focal word in the hero duplicated in pink behind, offset 2-3px down-right. Once per page.
- Body: Literata 17px, lineHeight 1.55, blue; labels medium a step smaller.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 2; no shadow, depth is overprint. Hairlines 1px #bfceec; 1.5px blue framing a figure.
- Grain: speck pattern fill at 8-15% on fills, bands, photos; never under running text.

## Controls
- Primary: fill #fe419d, text #061b64, radius 2, bold 15px, padding [14,24].
- Secondary: 1.5px blue stroke, blue text. Input: 48 tall, radius 2, 1.5px blue stroke.

## Range
- Figures as overprint: blue number at display size with a solid pink shape multiplied across part of it.
- Steps as separations: blue layer, pink layer, both: three plates in hairline frames.
- Bands in one drum: solid blue with stock-coloured statement, or solid pink with deep blue type.

## Voice
Specific and hand-set: name the ink, sheet, price; verbs over adjectives.
- Never black or grey.
- Never a smooth ungrained gradient.
- Never pink text on the stock.
