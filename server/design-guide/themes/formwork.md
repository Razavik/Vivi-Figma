# Formwork
A site-office voice: warm grey concrete paper, near-black ink, bold condensed capitals, square cast edges, hard shadows and one safety orange. Heavy and exact; the photographs show concrete in black and white so orange is the only colour.

## Palette
- paper #adaaa5 (page) | paper-2 #9e9b95 | paper-3 #8f8c85 (row hairlines)
- plate #c6c4bf | plate-ink #f1eeea (type on ink band)
- ink #111114 | ink-2 #2a2926 | muted #34312d (on paper and paper-2 only) | rule #8f8c85
- accent safety orange #f65311 (primary, one figure; under 5%) | deep #a72900 (button edge) | focus #8c2000 | accent-ink #111114
Balance: concrete grey with ink; max two bands (paper-2 or one ink band with paper type); never an orange field.

## Type
- Display: Oswald bold, uppercase, ls -0.5%, lh 1.0; hero 120-200; section heads 56-80; a lone figure up to 320. One colour per head.
- Body: Archivo regular/medium 16-17, lh 1.5, ink-2. Figures: Archivo Narrow medium, tabular, for measurements and counts.
- Labels/captions: Archivo medium 13-14, sentence case. Display caps never lowercase or light.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 0. Rules: 3px ink across a section/table top; 1px #8f8c85 between rows.
- Plate: fill #c6c4bf, 3px ink border, hard shadow {6,6,0,#111114}; one or two per page; nothing blurred.

## Controls
- Primary: fill #f65311, text #111114, Oswald bold caps 18, radius 0, h52, hard {0,3,0,#a72900} edge.
- Secondary: pressed in, 2px ink border (inner shadow {0,2,3,#11111440}).

## Range
- Figure cast as a plate: the prompt's one real count in Oswald 220-320 on a plate with the hard shadow, unit beside in Archivo.
- Comparison as two plates: two audiences or jobs, rows on hairlines; orange only on the one action.

## Voice
- A foreman's plain talk: what was poured, where, for whom, when it opens; short lines, every figure with its unit.
- Never: rounded corners, blurred shadows, a second accent; drawn concrete (seams, cracks, hazard stripes); colour photos beside the orange; lowercase or light Oswald.
