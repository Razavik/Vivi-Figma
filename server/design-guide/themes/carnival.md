# Carnival
A loud screen-printed register: tinted paper, deep warm ink, narrow poster capitals, a hard ink shadow and two accents that compete. Every surface is a flat fill with a hard edge, like a print pinned to a wall; the loudest thing is a fact in capitals at poster size.

## Palette
Default drop "cold-snap" (music, winter, vinyl):
- paper #ffddca | paper-2 #f5cebd | paper-3 #e7b8a7
- ink #2c0003 | ink-2 #421c19 | muted #66433d
- accent mustard #f6ce00 (fills primary control, a band, a well) | accent-ink #2c0003
- accent-2 oxblood #9a0000 (rules, links, focus, the other fill, with paper text) | rule #9a0000
Balance: two accents, one fills a surface, the next fills the next; never both inside one surface, never a gradient. Max two bands (an accent field or paper-2). Other drops swap the three roles (paper/accent/accent-2), e.g. lime + magenta, cherry + navy.

## Type
- Display: Big Shoulders Display extrabold, uppercase, ls +1.5%, lh 1.0; hero up to 160, section head 56-72, item title 32. Lone nowrap word may go lh 0.9.
- Body: Golos Text regular/medium 17, ink, sentence case. Labels medium 14.
- Figures: a selling fact (price, count) in the display face at hero size, tabular, unit beside in the same caps.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 2. Rules 2px: accent-2 between sections/rows; ink around a shadowed surface.
- Shadow {x:4,y:4,blur:0,color:#2c0003} on wells, controls, photos; nothing else casts depth.

## Controls
- Primary: fill #f6ce00, text #2c0003, display caps 20, 2px ink border, hard shadow, radius 2, padding [14,28].
- Secondary: paper fill, ink outline.

## Range
- Figures on a whole accent field: 3-4 facts in poster caps, each over a Golos line, parted by 2px ink bars.
- Statement on accent-2 under paper type, line at poster size across ten columns, one action beside it.

## Voice
- Loud, short, funny by being exact: count, price, date, one joke a screen. Caps heads of six words or fewer ending in a full stop.
- Never: soft shadows; sentence-case heads or loose tracking; grey/ghost buttons; drawn mascots, flames, skulls.
