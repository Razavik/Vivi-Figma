# Sport
A results-sheet material: cool concrete paper, blue-black ink, condensed capitals over a sans body, big condensed figures and mono times, hairlines and one heavier bar rule, volt as the only colour. Volt is a fill with ink on it, never a letter on paper.

## Palette
- paper #f6f9fc (page) | paper-2 #ebeff2 | paper-3 #dfe3e7
- ink #00082e | ink-2 #071a36 | muted #5b646f | rule #b4b8bc | rule-2 #273e5c (bar rule)
- accent #c8e928 (volt: fill only, under 5% of a screen) | accent-ink #00082e | accent-deep #678802 (link underline)
Balance: cool paper and ink, one volt fill; bands paper-2 or one ink band with paper type, never volt.

## Type
- Display: condensed extra-bold, UPPERCASE, upright, hero 80-128, lineHeight 1.02, letterSpacing -1.5%.
- Figures (distance, price, date): the condensed face, tabular, 56+, label in sans beneath. Live data (times, splits): JetBrains Mono medium.
- Body: Albert Sans 16-17px; labels/units medium a step smaller, muted, sentence case.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 4 on controls, inputs, photos; no pills. No shadows, gradients (except photo scrim) or texture.
- Rules: 1px #b4b8bc for rows; 3px #273e5c bar rule opens/closes a table and breaks a section. No boxed cards; a well is paper-2 under a bar rule.
- Photo duotone, 4px radius; type on it is paper over an ink scrim; a volt figure may hold a corner.

## Controls
- Primary: volt block #c8e928, ink text, radius 4, condensed caps, padding [14,24].
- Secondary: 2px ink stroke, radius 4. Input: radius 4, 1px #b4b8bc stroke.

## Range
- Race in figures: 3-4 numbers in condensed caps at display size under the bar rule.
- Results sheet: bar rule opening and closing a table of bibs and times in mono, followed runner's row a paper step lighter with the latest split in volt.
- Course as stages: numbered stages on one bar rule, caps title and one line each, live stage on volt.

## Voice
Race-briefing plain: distance, time, place; imperative caps heads.
- Never slant the display.
- Never volt type or a second accent.
- Never medal, trophy or podium imagery.
