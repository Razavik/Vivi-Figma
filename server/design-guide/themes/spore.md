# Spore
A cellar at night: coffee-ground black warmed toward brown, parchment serif heads, soft pills, photographs lit only by lamps and candles, one oyster-mushroom pink. Warmth sits in ground, grain, figures and one soft colour, never drawn light; not a dark template with a glow.

## Palette
- paper #0f0a06 (page) | paper-2 #19120e (grained well) | paper-3 #271d17 (band)
- ink #efe7d8 | ink-2 #d3c9bb | muted #9d9388 | rule #372f28
- accent #e89f92 (pink: primary fill, chip, price) | accent-2 #f6c3ba (focus, lighter) | accent-ink #0f0a06
Balance: three brown-black steps, parchment ink, one pink; no blue-black.

## Type
- Display: Young Serif regular, hero 72-112, lineHeight 1.02, letterSpacing -1.2%, sentence case. One weight: hierarchy by size, never bold.
- Figures: same serif, lining; one number may reach 150-300; a price is the one figure in pink.
- Body: Instrument Sans 16-17px, lineHeight 1.55, ink-2; a second name (vintage, Latin) in italic; labels medium 14px muted.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius: wells and photos 14, pills 999, inputs square. Rules 1px #372f28; fields 1.5px #d3c9bb.
- Depth through the three ground steps; the live row lifts one step with a pink chip. Shadow {0,16,40,#0f0a0666} only under a well over a photo.
- Grain: speck pattern ~15% on wells and bands only.

## Controls
- Primary: pink pill, fill #e89f92, text #0f0a06, padding [14,28].
- Secondary: pill, 1.5px #d3c9bb stroke. Chosen chip fills pink. Input: square, 1.5px edge.

## Range
- One figure as the section: count or price at 2-4x display size on a grained well, label beneath, 2-3 hairline rows.
- Week as timetable: day names in serif, hours beside, closed days muted, tonight lifted to paper-3 with a pink chip.
- Price list: ruled rows, name in serif, second name italic, price flush right in pink.

## Voice
A maker's log: name, day, hour, vintage, price. No wellness or mystique.
- Never a second hue.
- Never drawn light (beam, pool, glow).
- Never pink running text.
