# Bloom
Daylight on a working sheet: warm off-white paper, the medium's own grid (piano roll, timeline) printed faintly into the ground, umber ink. Making happens on the grid in rounded clips; touchable parts are physical (rubber cap, cased parts lit from above).

## Palette
- paper #f9f4ee | paper-2 #f3ece4 | paper-3 #ebe1d8 | sheet #fdfaf6 (stage)
- ink #221510 | ink-2 #3a2d27 | muted #6a5e56 | rule #ded6ce
- accent terracotta #af4529 (link, rubber, a lane; never a field) | accent-2 #c18154 | accent-ink #fef9f4
- lanes: #765e4e #8f433b #ca9246 #bd7981 | rubber #b84930 (hi #d17652, lo #872e21) | umber #3b2921 | bezel #ede5dd | case #efe8e1
- grid lines: ink at 4% / 8% / 15% alpha
Balance: warm paper; two quiet blooms only (terracotta ~8% alpha top right, #c18154 ~5% bottom left, blurred ellipses). Two bands max, never terracotta.

## Type
- Display: Ranade semibold, hero 64-96 / lh 1.06 / ls -2.4%, sentence case, ink.
- Body: regular 17-18, lh 1.55; medium for labels/controls (14-15, muted).
- Serif moment: Gambarino regular, once per page, 160px+ figure or one lede; never a head, never italic.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius card 18, inner 10, clip 6, pill 999; hairline 1px #ded6ce.
- Shadows: card {0,18,36,#2215103d} + {0,1,2,#2215100f}; lift {0,36,72,#7a3f2557}. Raised warm surfaces, no hairline boxes.
- Grid: stacked 1px lines (bar 15%, beat 8%, row 5%) on #fdfaf6, fading out away from the stage (gradient mask).
- Clips: rounded 6 rectangles in lane colours, lit top edge, inset hairline, waveform bars inside.

## Controls
- Primary: rubber cap (ellipse, radial gradient #d17652 to #b84930 to #872e21) in a bezel well #ede5dd, at the left of a #fdfaf6 pill with label, medium 16.

## Range
- Input becoming output on one grid: trace settling onto lanes of clips below.
- Choices as cased spines: stacked cases with colour band and pips; chosen one slid out.
- One serif figure beside three steps, each with a small grid crop.

## Voice
- Warm, daylight, specific: the verb, the output, where.
- Never: dark canvas or neon glow; terracotta fields; full-strength grid across a section; italic or doubled serif.
