# Squeeze
A market stall in colour: a forest-green ground with real chroma, warm white ink, round heavy heads and figures, soft tiles a step lighter or deeper, bright fruit photos and juicy peach controls. A price or day set huge is as much the material as the fruit. Not a neutral grocery template with a green button.

## Palette
- paper #075930 (page, whole surface) | paper-2 #1b6b3e (tiles, bands) | paper-3 #004523 (sunk well, deeper band)
- ink #fdfcf8 | ink-2 #e9eddf | muted #d1e4d4 | rule #3c8256
- accent #ffb37c (peach: controls only) | accent-2 #f68953 (pressed) | accent-ink #4a2010 | focus #ffd060
- card #f9f5ec | card-ink #032617 (the one warm-white card per page, for what is paid for)
Balance: drenched green; peach on controls only; never white, grey or peach bands.

## Type
- Display: Gabarito extra-bold, hero 72-120, lineHeight 1.0, letterSpacing -2.5%, sentence case, never below bold.
- Figures: same face, tabular; one figure may be 3-4x the head size at -4%.
- Body: Manrope medium 16-17px, lineHeight 1.55, muted; links and row titles bold. Labels: bold, a step smaller, sentence case.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Tiles: paper-2, radius 24, no border or shadow; chosen tile sinks to paper-3 with a 3px peach ring.
- Card: #f9f5ec, radius 24, shadow {0,14,32,#00452380}. Rules 2px #3c8256; 1px only between list rows.
- Photos radius 24, no ring; flat green, no texture.

## Controls
- Primary: fill #ffb37c, text #4a2010, radius 16, Manrope bold, padding [16,28].
- Secondary: 2px ink stroke, radius 16. Chips pills, chosen one peach. Input: 2px #3c8256 stroke, radius 16.

## Range
- One figure as the screen: price, size or day at statement size straight on green, head and a line beside it.
- Timetable of dates as tiles: date huge, weekday in label style, chosen one on paper-3 with a peach ring.
- Promise as statement on the deeper green band, display size across ten columns, one action beneath.

## Voice
The stall-holder's: fruit, day it comes, what ripe feels like; price said plainly.
- Never a neutral ground; white only for the one card.
- Never a dull greyed green.
- Never a weight under medium or a mono figure.
