# Paste-up
A hand-made flyer: kraft board, near-black toner, tall condensed capitals at poster size over a book serif, white copy paper lifted on real paper shadows, controls on hard ink offsets, one sheet of fluorescent yellow. The paste-up lives in the paper, the tilt and the lift, never in props.

## Palette
- paper (kraft board) #C4A179 | paper-2 #B08A62 (deeper kraft) | paper-3 #F7F5EF (copy sheet)
- ink (toner) #130E0B | ink-2 #241E19 | muted #32261C | rule #785D41 (on board) | rule-2 #CECAC2 (on sheet)
- accent #ECF423 (fluorescent sheet: the primary control fill and one sheet per screen; a paper, never type or a word on the board) | accent-ink #130E0B
Balance: kraft board, white sheets, toner type; max two bands: paper-2 or toner with board-coloured type.

## Type
- Display: League Gothic-style condensed, UPPERCASE, letterSpacing 1%, never under 28px; flyer head 120-200 at lineHeight 0.92, section head 64-96 at 1.0. Item titles (h3) in Newsreader medium instead.
- Body: Newsreader regular/medium 18, lineHeight 1.5; italic only for emphasis. On a sheet the text sits on copy white; on the board use ink, never muted, under 18px.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- radius 0 (pills 999 allowed for chips). Sheets shadow {0,4,10,#18100C1F}; raised {0,14,30,#18100C42}.
- tilt: 0.5°-2° rotation on at most three surfaces a screen; inputs, tables and long text stay square.
- rules: 1px #785D41 on board, #CECAC2 on sheets.

## Controls
- primary: fill #ECF423, text #130E0B, Newsreader medium 15, hard shadow {4,4,0,#130E0B}.
- secondary: fill #F7F5EF, same hard offset.

## Range
- The facts as a wall of flyers: date, count, price, deadline each on its own sheet at poster size, mixed spans and heights, overlapping a few px, two or three tilted, one fluorescent; no photograph.
- A typed sheet: timetable, price list or where-and-when as a ruled table on one copy sheet at a slight tilt, left column condensed, rest serif.

## Voice
- Flyer voice: the date, the door, the price, the table number.
- Never: props (tape, stickers, stamps, scrawled notes); a cut-out, yellow or italic word inside a head; soft shadows on controls; grunge overlays, torn edges or colour photos.
