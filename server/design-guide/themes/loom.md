# Loom
A weaving studio speaking quietly: ecru paper, a book-calm display serif over a plain grotesk, thin rules, and one deep indigo, the colour of dyed yarn, as a band or a button and never a wash. It reads crafted because it is ruled like a pattern book.

## Palette
- paper #F4F0E6 (page) | paper-2 #ECE4D5 (bands, wells) | paper-3 #D9CCB7 (sand, deeper wells)
- ink #1D1713 | ink-2 #352F2A | muted #5E5650 | rule #CEC6B8 | ink rule #1D1713
- accent #1D3172 (indigo: one band, the primary, a line) | accent-2 #314A94 (primary hover) | accent-ink #F4F0E6 | focus #2B46A1
Balance: ecru and ink; max two bands: paper-2 or one indigo band (ecru text).

## Type
- Display: Caslon-style roman, regular only, no italic, no faux bold; headings and any price/figure at 40px+, hero 64-104, lineHeight 1.04, letterSpacing -1%, sentence case.
- Body: Hanken Grotesk regular 16-18, lineHeight 1.5.
- Labels/captions/tags/table heads: Hanken Grotesk medium one step smaller, sentence case, untracked, muted. Mono only for comparable figures (cm, g/m, metres).

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- radius 0 everywhere. Shadow none; depth is a paper step.
- rules: 1px #CEC6B8 between rows; 2px ink above a list's first row, under a table head, under the nav. No borders on wells.
- photos: square, inset stroke ink at 8%. No texture.

## Controls
- primary: square fill #1D3172, text #F4F0E6, Hanken medium, h 48, padding [0,24]; on the indigo band it turns ecru with indigo text.
- secondary: text link with 2px bottom border in text colour.

## Range
- Specification as ruled rows: label in grotesk left, value in serif at title size or larger, the price row largest; 2px ink rule above row one.
- Price as a figure: price per metre in the serif at display size beside the sum in rows (metres, rate, total), on a sand well or the indigo band.

## Voice
- The weaver speaking plainly: fibre, metre, weeks, the room the cloth is for. Prices and times said once; nothing sold hard.
- Never: indigo as a page wash or tint; a mono label voice; drawn rulers, warps or thread patterns; warm accents beside the ecru; radius, shadow or soft cards.
