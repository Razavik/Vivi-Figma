# Ribbon
The hour before sleep, set quietly: light upright heads, soft grey reading text, hairlines instead of boxes, slow light behind well-made machined parts. Not a glossy wellness app, no stars or moons.

## Palette
- paper #fcf9f3 (page) | paper-2 #f5f1e9 | paper-3 #eae5db
- ink #13110f | muted #5e5a53 | rule #e0dcd4
- accent #035f5c (the one lit thing: primary, one figure, fader glow) | accent-ink #faf8f4 | accent-soft #d1ebe9 (fader track, never type)
- band #002b2b (dark teal ground for the drawn night) | band-ink #efebe2 | metal #f7f5f1 to #d1cdc7
Balance: light paper, one teal signal; the dark band carries the hero night.

## Type
- Display: Instrument Sans regular (medium for small titles), hero 72-112, lineHeight 1.04, letterSpacing -3.5%, sentence case, one colour; big figures too.
- Body: Figtree 17px regular, lineHeight 1.6, muted; medium for part names. Labels: Figtree medium 14px, muted.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius: card 20, part 6, controls pill. Rules 1px #e0dcd4, no boxes.
- Panels: gradient #f7f5f1 to #d1cdc7 top-down, 1px white top highlight, shadow {0,24,48,#13110f4D}; parts {0,10,18,#13110f4D}.
- Night: gradient on #002b2b with thin soft light streaks and a row of small lit points on a low horizon; soft grain only on band and panels.

## Controls
- Primary: pill 52 tall, fill #035f5c, text #faf8f4, inner highlight, padding [16,28], medium.
- Secondary: pill, metal gradient, 1px #e0dcd4. Switch: pill track, metal knob.

## Range
- Instrument hero: product controls as a machined panel (recessed bed, vertical faders, teal index line) beside a one-line head over the night.
- List of levels: rows on hairlines, name in display face, level as a display figure.
- Price under the horizon: figure 128-160 over the night band, one line, lit pill.

## Voice
Calm and specific: the hour, the sound, how loud. Never a promise of perfect sleep.
- Never italic or heavy heads.
- Never starfield, moon or navy-violet gradient.
- Never a drawn waveform.
