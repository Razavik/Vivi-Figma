# Ultraviolet
A producer's tool in one pigment: lavender paper tinted the same hue as the ink, one saturated violet in two strengths, lowercase geometric heads, pills throughout, depth held to a hairline, photographs pulled into the violet. Not a purple-gradient startup page, not a neon club flyer.

## Palette
- paper #f0ecfa (page) | paper-2 #e7e1f6 | paper-3 #ded6ef
- ink #131019 | ink-2 #2b2636 | muted #5c5270 | rule #cec5e1 | rule-2 #a295cb (pill outlines)
- accent #6b2ae3 (the one pigment) | accent-deep #571dbc (pressed) | accent-soft #d4c7ff (unplayed waveform) | accent-ink #f6f4fb
Balance: lavender paper, one violet; bands paper-2, ink, or one violet field (white pill on it), two at most. No second chromatic colour, even in photos.

## Type
- Display: Outfit medium, lowercase always (heads, nav, buttons), hero 72-112, lineHeight 1.0, letterSpacing -3%. Figures: Outfit light at display size, lowercase units.
- Body: Onest 16-17px, lineHeight 1.55, sentence case. Labels: Outfit medium a step smaller, lowercase, muted.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius: pill 999 on every control and tag; surfaces, screens, photos 16; nothing square.
- Hairlines 1px #cec5e1; outlined pills 1.5px #a295cb. No card borders: a surface is a step to paper-2. Raised shadow {0,1,2,#1310190F}. No blur, glow, gradient or texture.
- Waveforms are line work: played part violet, rest #d4c7ff.

## Controls
- Primary: violet pill #6b2ae3, text #f6f4fb, Outfit medium lowercase, padding [14,28]; on a violet field it turns white with violet text.
- Secondary: pill, 1.5px ink stroke. Input: pill, 1.5px #a295cb stroke.

## Range
- Lanes as the picture: product waveforms across 10-12 columns, one stem per row under hairlines, bar ruler above.
- Figures in light Outfit at display size parted by hairlines, one in violet.
- Table of uses: lowercase table, use at title size, stems as small pills, one line of what you hear.

## Voice
Lowercase, short, exact; a producer, not a label.
- Never a capital in a head, nav link or button.
- Never a second chromatic colour.
- Never a purple-pink gradient, glow or square control.
