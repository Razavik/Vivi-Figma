# Dose
Translucent plastic and daylight: warm morning paper, bands of soft tinted plastic like a pill organiser, green-black ink, and mono for the time and dose set like a pharmacy label. One pharmacy green does every signal. Exact and unhurried; a kitchen, not a clinic.

## Palette
- paper #faf7ef | paper-2 #e9f8ef | paper-3 #d4f0e0
- ink #0c1c16 | ink-2 #26342f | muted #54655d | rule #cbd6c9
- accent green #007948 (controls, a stamp, one figure) | deep #005f3a | soft mint #a9e8c6 (checked) | accent-ink #f8fdfa
- plastic tints (never a state): mon #eec1bf | tue #f2cfaf | wed #eee4b4 | thu #a8e2c2 | fri #b1dde6 | sat #bfcae9 | sun #dac1de
- light #fffdf1 | glint #ffffff
Balance: warm paper; tints carry colour; mint = checked; green only on signals. One ink band (#0c1c16) may carry a statement.

## Type
- Display: Schibsted Grotesk bold, hero 64-88, lh 1.02, ls -3%, tabular lining figures.
- Body: Instrument Sans regular/medium 16-18, lh 1.5; labels medium 13-14.
- Times, doses, labels: DM Mono regular/medium (08:00, 500 mg), inline or as a timetable's time column; never display figures.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius controls 10, surfaces 14, tags 999, phone screen 28.
- Plastic: tint fill at 62% alpha, 1px #ffffff top line, inner shade at the foot (inner shadow {0,-6,8,#2a4a3a1f}), and a blurred tint-coloured glow below on the paper (blur 24).
- Cards lift {8,18,28,#3e5c4a52} plus {0,1,1,#2a3b3319}.

## Controls
- Moulded plastic: h52, radius 10, 1px rim, 3px darker bottom edge, #ffffff top bevel, soft shadow. Primary green plastic with #f8fdfa label; secondary clear.

## Range
- Sequence as plastic bands: each stage a full-width tinted band, number at display size, two phone screens, minute in DM Mono.
- Record as labels: photo beside two pharmacy labels, one stamped green "given", one grey "missed".

## Voice
- A pharmacist's plainness spoken by a family: name, dose, time, who gave it; never a journey or a red warning.
- Never: red/amber for missed; tint as a signal or brand colour; clinical stock imagery; glossy toy plastic.
