# Sticker
A print shop's material: warm off-white sheet, warm black ink, heavy capitals, hot pink and lime fills, every raised surface edged in a thick white die-cut on a hard offset shadow. Loud and handmade because depth is printed and colours are fills, never tints. Not soft pastel, not a cartoon toy page.

## Palette
- paper #f5f0e7 (page) | paper-2 #efe6d8 | paper-3 #fffdf8
- ink #1b1813 | ink-2 #312d27 | muted #6a6159 | rule #e4dccf
- white #fefdfb (die-cut border) | accent #d3036a (pink: fills that act, price) | accent-ink #fefdfb
- accent-2 #cae649 (lime: fills that mark, tags, counts)
Balance: thirds: pink acts, lime marks, ink types. Bands paper-2, ink, or one lime field; two at most.

## Type
- Display: Bowlby One SC, UPPERCASE only, hero <=72, section heads <=52, lineHeight 1.02. Never a paragraph. Figures in it; one lone figure per screen on a fill may reach 190.
- Body: Sofia Sans 16-17px, lineHeight 1.5. Labels/tags bold a step smaller, sentence case, muted.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Die-cut: raised surfaces (card, fact fill, tag, photo, primary) get a 6px white border and hard shadow {5,6,0,#1b1813}. Tables and bands stay borderless, parted by a dashed 2px #e4dccf rule.
- Radius: card 18, photo 22, controls pill; tables and inputs square. Raised surfaces tilt -6 to 6 deg, alternating. No texture or gradient.

## Controls
- Primary: pink pill #d3036a, text #fefdfb, 5px white ring plus hard shadow, padding [16,32].
- Secondary: ghost pill, 2px ink stroke. Chips: chosen one ink-filled. Input: 2px ink stroke, radius 18.

## Range
- Facts as die-cut fills: bento of pink, lime, ink, white tiles, one fact each, tilted a degree or two alternately; the lead tile holds the lone figure.
- Steps as stuck-down cards: 3-4 cards, numeral on a lime disc, tilted against each other, overlapping a dashed rule.
- Statement on the ink band with one pink or lime tag across a corner carrying the fact.

## Voice
Cheerful and exact: price, quantity, size, ship day.
- Never a blurred shadow.
- Never a die-cut border under 4px or not white.
- Never pink text on paper; never a drawn mascot.
