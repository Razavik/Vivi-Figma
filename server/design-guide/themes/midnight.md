# Midnight
A cold, calm night instrument whose blue is the ground itself: deep midnight blue to every edge, cool white ink, light wide heads like film titles, surfaces cut square in steps of lighter blue, one azure signal. Photographs arrive as moonlight.

## Palette
- paper #02071D (ground) | paper-2 #071129 | paper-3 #0F1C37
- ink #E7F0F9 | ink-2 #BDDCF7 | muted #98A9C1 | rule #1C2A48 | rule-2 #364868
- accent #6BCBF7 (azure: primary, one meter value, a figure) | accent-2 #3483AD (meter fill) | accent-ink #040C22 | focus #6BDAFF
Balance: whole canvas dark blue; azure under 5% of a screen; max two paper-2/paper-3 bands; never an azure field or a light band.

## Type
- Display: Mona Sans expanded, weight 300, hero 72-112, lineHeight 1.04, letterSpacing -1.5%, sentence case; a long head steps down in size rather than tightening. Item titles medium at normal width.
- Figures that are the point: display cut 300, tabular, 96-200, letterSpacing -3%, unit beside it at normal width regular in ink-2.
- Body: normal width regular 16-18, lineHeight 1.6.
- Labels/table heads/meta: medium one step smaller, sentence case, muted. No mono.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- radius 0 everywhere. Depth = lightness steps paper, paper-2, paper-3; no outline around surfaces.
- rules: 1px #1C2A48 only between table/list rows.
- shadow for a lifted surface: {0,24,48,#060D2AB3}. No glass, blur, glow, bloom or lit edges. No texture.
- photos: square, 1px inside stroke ink at 8%.

## Controls
- primary: square azure fill #6BCBF7, text #040C22, medium, h 48, padding [0,24].
- secondary: square, 1px #364868 stroke, ink text.

## Range
- One real number as the statement: display cut at up to twice hero size, alone on the ground with unit and one line.
- A cropped interface detail on a paper-2 well (job sheet, meter, contact sheet), never in a drawn window.

## Voice
- Cool and spare, night shift: the hour, the count, the cost. Short declaratives; a number always carries unit or time. No hype.
- Never: a warm accent; heavy display weight or normal-width heads; radial glow, halo or lit card edge; rounded corners or light sections; daylight or full-colour photos.
