# Lumen
A night console with a literary voice: violet-black ground, near-white ink, lowercase serif heads a step large over a plain sans, hairline wells and one molten brass signal. Cool dark, warm light: warmth is rationed to the brass and one rose verb. The product is the picture.

## Palette
- paper #060510 (ground) | paper-2 #0D0B19 (bands, wells) | paper-3 #161324
- ink #F3F0F8 | ink-2 #CFCCD8 | muted #9C98AA | rule #262436 | rule-2 #3D3951
- accent #FFA54B (brass: primary fill, one line or readout; under 5% of a screen) | accent-ink #090714
- accent-2 #F3798B (rose: the one verb in the hero head only) | live #72D699 (live state only)
Balance: violet-black everywhere; max two paper-2 bands; no accent field, no violet field or gradient.

## Type
- Display: Instrument Serif regular, roman, ALL LOWERCASE, hero 96-144, section heads 56-80 (never plain section size, it reads thin), lineHeight 0.95-1.02, letterSpacing -1.5%.
- Body: Geist regular/medium 16-18, tabular figures.
- Labels/captions/table heads: Geist regular one step smaller, lowercase, muted.
- The verb: one accented word in the hero head, in #F3798B with a 1px underline.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- radius: surfaces 10, pills 999.
- rules: 1px #262436 on wells, rows, inputs; #3D3951 on control edges.
- depth = lightness steps paper to paper-3; a floating surface shadow {0,28,64,#04030C CC}.
- texture: blueprint grid, 1px lines at 5% alpha on a 48px pitch (`pattern`), inside one well or band only, never under text. No glass or blur.

## Controls
- primary: pill fill #FFA54B, text #090714, lowercase, h 44-48.
- secondary: pill with 1px #3D3951 stroke, ink text.

## Range
- A path as a diagram: nodes and connectors in hairlines; the slow path muted, the product's path ruled in brass.
- A request beside its response in one well split by a hairline.

## Voice
- Engineered, plain, specific: model, state, unit, price. Like a lab-book note; no exclamation marks.
- Never: a drawn or generated light (orb, ring, halo); a violet field or gradient; a coloured noun or a second accented word; a warm ground or third warm colour.
