# Werkstatt
A design school's printed prospectus: warm putty ground, near-black ink, heavy geometric capitals, square 2px and 3px ink rules, and three flat primaries, blue, red and yellow, each with one job. Photos are black and white records of the shops.

## Palette
- paper #d2c9b9 (page) | paper-2 #c6bba8 (band) | paper-3 #e2dccf (well inside a cell)
- ink #14110e | ink-2 #2a2522 | muted #4b433e | rule #83786c
- blue #1949c5 | red #d93411 (deep #b71c07) | yellow #f4c929 (ink text) | accent-ink #f6f1e9
Balance: putty and ink; primaries are flat fills only, never type or tints; each codes one category, same wherever it appears; a set of one kind takes no colour. Bands paper-2, ink, or one blue field; two at most.

## Type
- Display: Jost bold, UPPERCASE, hero 72-112, lineHeight 1.02, letterSpacing -0.5%. Numerals and dates in it at display size, tabular, inside their cell.
- Body: Karla 16-17px, lineHeight 1.5. Labels semibold a step smaller, sentence case, muted. Controls: Karla semibold UPPERCASE.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 0, no shadow, gradient, texture or card backgrounds: a cell is what the rules enclose.
- Rules: 3px ink under nav and over tables; 2px ink boxing a grid; 1px #83786c between rows.
- Photos B/W, square, 3px ink border, flush with cells; the shop's primary runs as a flat cell or bar beside the picture and carries the caption.

## Controls
- Primary: ink-filled square, paper text, Karla semibold caps, padding [14,24]. On an ink band it turns putty with ink text.
- Secondary: transparent, 3px ink stroke. Input: square, 3px ink stroke.

## Range
- Figures in boxed cells: real numbers in the display face, each in its own 2px-ruled cell across twelve columns, label beneath, no primary.
- Dates as ruled schedule: rows of caps as large as a section head, day left, event right, 3px rule over the first row.
- Categories as coded grid: each shop in its primary cell flush with neighbours, B/W photo above the fill.

## Voice
Plain school prose: shop, week, tuition, deadline, each said once.
- Never a fourth colour, tint, primary as type, or colour photograph.
- Never a radius on anything.
- Never mixed-case display.
