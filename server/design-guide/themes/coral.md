# Coral
A warm plain product voice made physical: fine stationery on a felt desk, one daylight from the left. Records are cotton sheets, envelopes and index cards with real edges; controls are letterpress cards; one coral ink is pressed in where something is settled.

## Palette
- paper #f8f4f1 | paper-2 #f1ece8 | paper-3 #e8e1dd | desk #f0ebe7 (hero ground)
- cotton #fdfbf8 (stationery) | print #221b19 (type on stock)
- ink #1d1614 | ink-2 #312926 | muted #615955 | rule #d9d3cf | rule-2 #c7bfba
- accent coral #ef8b7b (only the pressed settled state, underlines) | accent-2 #bb3d31 (focus) | paid #ba4a34 (settled word) | accent-ink #1d1614
- board #1f1917 with board-type #f6f3ef (one dark band, max)
Balance: warm grey paper, cotton objects, coral rare.

## Type
- Display: General Sans semibold, hero 64-88, lh 1.05, ls -3%, sentence case. A lone price 2-3x display size, ls -5%, lh 0.85, unit beside at 24 medium.
- Body: regular/medium 16-17. Labels: medium 13-14, muted. Figures in columns: Geist Mono 400.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius card 8, input 6, pill 999. Hairline 1px #d9d3cf.
- One light from the left: every paper object has a 1px lit top/left edge (#ffffff at 90%), a 0.5px shade outline, and a cast shadow falling right {6,5,10,#4d3a2e4d}; lifted {12,10,18,#4d3a2e4d}.
- Settled stamp: word in semibold #ba4a34, rotated 2-3 degrees, ~85% opacity.
- Groove (choice of 2-4): radius 12, inner shadow top-left, cotton card riding in it.

## Controls
- Letterpress card, not pill: radius 8, h48, fill #fdfbf8, edge + cast shadow, label medium 16 in print colour with 1px lit shadow below.

## Range
- The job as paperwork on the desk: sheet, window envelope, reply card, outcome stamped.
- Records as an index: cotton index cards stacked with offset tabs beside the full sheet.
- Price on the board: huge lone figure in paper beside a ruled list; action on an envelope flap or card.

## Voice
- Plain, warm second person about work and money: who, how much, when; never cute.
- Never: cool paper or blue signal; a second light direction; pill or flat square buttons; coral as a band, fill behind text or head.
