# Rim
An instrument at night: warm black paper, off-white ink, medium-weight heads, mono for every measured number, square surfaces parted by hairlines, an orange signal and a sky-blue second. Exact because every edge is hard and every time carries its zone; not a glowing sci-fi page.

## Palette
- paper #030201 (page) | paper-2 #080604 (lifted well) | paper-3 #010100 (sunk well)
- ink #efebe4 | ink-2 #cecac3 | muted #9c9890 | rule #24211c | rule-2 #0f0d09
- accent #f97c3d (action, current state) | accent-ink #070201
- accent-2 #6db0f4 (second state, time window)
Balance: near-black, off-white ink, orange and blue as small signals only. Bands paper-2/3, two at most, never light.

## Type
- Display: Switzer medium, hero 72-112, lineHeight 1.02, letterSpacing -4% (section heads -3%), sentence case. Never bold.
- Body: Switzer 16-17px regular, lineHeight 1.55, ink-2; labels medium a step smaller, muted.
- Measurements: Geist Mono regular for every time, coordinate, area, price. Times always carry the zone (UTC). Key figure 48-72, unit at half size muted. Mono never sets heads or labels.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 0 everywhere. Rules 1px #24211c above section heads, around wells; rows #0f0d09.
- Depth by lightness only; no shadow, blur or glow.
- Corner ticks: two 12px L-shapes, 1px muted, at opposite corners of a well or photo. Photo: 1px inset ink ring at 8%.

## Controls
- Primary: square, fill #f97c3d, text #070201, Switzer medium, padding [14,24].
- Secondary: square, 1px #24211c stroke. Input: square, 1px muted stroke, mono values.

## Range
- Measurements as figures: 2-4 real quantities in mono at display size, split by vertical hairlines, units half size.
- Diagram of real time: UTC axis in hairlines with ticks, window as 10px blue band, events as 1px orange pins with mono times.
- Product built: queue on a paper-2 well with corner ticks, chosen row on paper-3 with an orange square.

## Voice
Measured and exact: site, pass, time in UTC, area in km2. No wonder, no frontier.
- Never anything round.
- Never a time without its zone.
- Never bold display, planet, starfield or glow.
