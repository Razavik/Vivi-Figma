# Wayfind
Public signage set as a page: cool grey paper, blue-black ink, every glyph in a sober mono cut to a departure board's cell, square 2px ink borders, ink plates that carry a sign, cobalt for what is live and amber for figures.

## Palette
- paper #edf0f4 (page) | paper-2 #dde2e7 (band) | paper-3 #fbfeff (input)
- ink #0a0d13 | ink-2 #282b32 | muted #565b64 | rule #b9bec5 | ink-rule #34383f (rows on ink)
- accent #2055e1 (cobalt: what is live, primary action) | accent-ink #fcfeff
- accent-2 #f4b522 (amber: figures on ink, or a fill under ink text; never text on paper)
Balance: grey paper, ink borders and plates. Network line colours only as small squares beside names. Bands paper-2, ink, or one cobalt field, two at most.

## Type
- Display: mono extra-bold, hero <=76, lineHeight 0.98, letterSpacing -3%, sentence case.
- Body: mono regular 16-17px, lineHeight 1.55, tabular. Figures extra-bold at display-small+; amber on ink, ink on paper.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 0 except pills. Depth is borders: 2px ink on wells, plates, buttons, table head; rows 1px #b9bec5 (#34383f on ink).
- Plate: square ink field (or amber under ink text) in a 2px border holding one instruction and its figure.
- Photo square in a 2px ink border; type only on a plate inside the frame. No texture or gradient.

## Controls
- Primary: square, fill #2055e1, text #fcfeff, extra-bold, 48-52 tall.
- Secondary: transparent, 2px ink stroke. Choice set: square cells sharing 2px borders, chosen one ink. Input: square, 2px ink stroke.

## Range
- Departure board table on the ink band: destination medium, line colour square, minutes in amber extra-bold, a late row as amber fill under ink text.
- Route as steps: 3-4 bordered square plates in a row, one instruction and figure each, joined by a 2px ink rule.
- Status rows: swatch, name bold, ends regular, state at right edge; one cobalt dot on the changed row.

## Voice
Signage: platform, exit, minute, carriage. Imperative and short.
- Never a proportional face.
- Never amber text on paper.
- Never a third bright colour or a rounded card.
