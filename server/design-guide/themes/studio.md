# Studio
A design studio's proof sheet, caught mid-build: cool studio-white paper with a faint ink dot grid, blue-black ink, serif heads, crop marks at the corners of printed pieces, and one cadmium orange that fills the action and draws the construction (metric guides, clear-space boxes, redlines). Not warm cream with terracotta.

## Palette
- paper #f1f7f8 (page) | paper-2 #e5edef | paper-3 #d8e2e4 | paper-hi #f9fcfd (sheet)
- ink #030e1a | ink-2 #2f3943 | muted #58626a | rule #c4cccf
- accent #e97219 (the one action fill; 1px construction lines) | accent-ink #030e1a
- dot grid and crop marks: ink at 15-25% alpha
Balance: cool paper and ink; never an orange flood or orange type. Bands paper-2 or one ink band.

## Type
- Display: Hedvig Letters Serif regular, wordmark 96-200, item titles 25, lineHeight 1.0, letterSpacing -2.2%. Heads, figures, wordmark only.
- Body: Geist 16-17px, lineHeight 1.55, ink-2; labels medium a step smaller, muted, tabular measurements.

## Shape and depth (Figma values)
- Radius 3. Rules 1px #c4cccf; 1px ink opens a set.
- Sheet shadow (paper objects only): {1,2,0,#030e1a12} + {10,18,36,#030e1a52}; all else flat.
- Dot grid: 24px pattern of small ink dots at ~15% on hero, nav, footer, fading out before the next section.
- Crop marks: eight 1px ink L-ticks set off each corner of a sheet, photo, card or control.
- Construction: 1px orange lines at ascender, cap height, x-height, baseline, descender with readouts.

## Controls
- Primary: fill #e97219, ink text, 1px ink border, second 1px rule inset 2px, corner ticks, Geist medium, 48 tall, padding [14,24].
- Field: radius 3, 1px muted stroke.

## Range
- Wordmark under construction: the name huge on the dot grid with metric guides, clear-space box and readouts.
- Specimen cards: 2-3 crop-marked cards (face line, Aa Gg Rr 0123456789, hex swatches).
- Size test: one line at falling sizes, each on its own orange guides with its size beside it.

## Voice
Spare, exact, a little literary; name the work, surface, measurement.
- Never the serif in running text.
- Never an orange flood or orange type.
- Never warm paper or guides untrue to the font.
